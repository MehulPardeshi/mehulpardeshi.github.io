Privacy-preserving healthcare LLM architecture (FCH)
2025 — 2026

Large language models over patient records invent clinical values and leak protected health information — unacceptable in a clinical setting. I designed a fact-curated architecture: a deterministic layer computes every clinical fact under role- and tenant-scoped access control, and the model is only ever asked to phrase the result in plain language.

It runs inside a live multi-tenant blood-bank platform serving thalassemia patients, their doctors, and the blood banks that manage their care — and it is shipped on Google Play.

MaverickAI

Founder & Product Lead
Oct 2024 — Sep 2025

Blunt automated blocking silences legitimate users, and small teams can't afford moderation tooling. I founded an open-source AI content-moderation tool and led 5–8 contributors, defining the product around the trade-offs that actually decide AI safety: false positives versus safety, automation versus human oversight, and explainability.

The human-override and escalation paths — letting moderators reverse a model decision — are what made it defensible. Selected for Kumbhathon incubation and published as a governance framework.
